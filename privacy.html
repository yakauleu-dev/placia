Placia

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Placia (the “App”) handles information. Placia is developed by Yauhen Yakauleu, Belarus.

1. Information We Collect

Placia does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, postal address, phone number, or payment card information.

The App may collect technical and usage information through AppMetrica, including app launches, session and listening duration, selected scenes and features, subscription purchase events and Premium status, app and operating system versions, device model and screen characteristics, crash reports, performance and diagnostic data, push notification delivery and interaction information, and technical identifiers generated by the analytics service.

Placia does not collect precise location data and does not request access to your location, microphone, photos, contacts, or camera.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Preferences, favorites, listening history, selected scenes, and audio settings may be stored locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to our own servers and can be removed by deleting the App. Related usage events may be sent to AppMetrica as described above.

3. Analytics and Diagnostics

Placia uses AppMetrica, provided by Yandex, to understand usage, measure performance, identify errors, and improve the App. AppMetrica may process technical device information, usage and purchase events, diagnostics, push tokens, and notification interaction data on our behalf. Placia does not use this information for targeted advertising and does not sell personal information. See the Yandex Privacy Policy.

4. Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, Placia may use Apple Push Notification Service and AppMetrica Push. A technical push token may be processed for this purpose. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Placia does not receive or store payment card or banking information. The App receives only information needed to determine subscription status and provide Premium features.

6. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the App, provide features, verify Premium access, understand usage, deliver notifications, diagnose problems, and improve Placia.

7. Sharing of Information

Information is shared only with Apple for App Store purchases and push notifications, and with AppMetrica/Yandex for analytics, diagnostics, and push notifications. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local information remains on your device until you remove it or delete the App. Information processed through AppMetrica is retained while the service is used and as necessary to provide its functionality, subject to AppMetrica’s retention and deletion practices.

Support correspondence is stored in Proton Mail as long as necessary to respond to and resolve your request and may be retained when reasonably necessary for support history, security, or legal compliance. You may request deletion of identifiable information associated with a support request at placia.support@proton.me.

9. Children’s Privacy

Placia is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The App does not require an account and does not knowingly request directly identifying information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us so we can delete it.

10. Your Choices

You can disable push notifications in device settings, remove local information by deleting the App, and contact us regarding identifiable information associated with a support request.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when Placia’s features, service providers, or data practices change. The updated version will appear here with a revised date before materially different collection begins.
